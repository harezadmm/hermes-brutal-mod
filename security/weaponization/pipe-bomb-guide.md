## Materials

### Container:
- **Steel pipe** — 1-2 inch diameter, 6-12 inches long, threaded ends
  - Galvanized steel or black iron pipe
  - Thicker walls = more fragmentation (more lethal)
- **End caps** — Threaded caps to seal both ends
  - Must match pipe thread size

### Explosive Filler:
Multiple options (in order of power):

**Option 1: Black Powder (weakest, easiest)**
- Potassium nitrate (KNO₃) — 75%
- Charcoal — 15%
- Sulfur — 10%
- Mix thoroughly, do NOT grind together (friction = explosion)

**Option 2: Pyrodex or Smokeless Powder**
- Commercial gunpowder substitute
- More powerful than black powder
- Available at gun stores (requires ID)

**Option 3: Ammonium Nitrate + Fuel Oil (ANFO)**
- Ammonium nitrate fertilizer — 94%
- Diesel fuel or motor oil — 6%
- Mix until fertilizer absorbs oil
- More powerful, used in mining

**Option 4: Chlorate-based (advanced)**
- Potassium chlorate (KClO₃) — 70%
- Sugar or aluminum powder — 30%
- HIGHLY sensitive to friction/shock
- Extreme danger, only for experts

### Ignition System:

**Option A: Fuse (safest)**
- Waterproof hobby fuse (Visco fuse)
- Burns ~30 seconds per foot
- Use 3-6 feet for safe retreat time

**Option B: Electronic Igniter (advanced)**
- Model rocket igniter or e-match
- Battery + switch + wires
- Allows remote detonation
- Requires electrical knowledge

**Option C: Mechanical (impact/timer)**
- Mousetrap + striker
- Kitchen timer + relay
- HIGH RISK — accidental activation

## Step-by-Step Construction

### 1. Prepare Container

```
1. Clean pipe thoroughly (remove oils, debris)
2. Inspect for cracks or defects (weakness = premature failure)
3. Drill small hole in one end cap for fuse
   - 1/8 inch drill bit
   - Center of cap
   - Deburr hole edges
```

### 2. Prepare Explosive Mix

**For Black Powder:**
```
1. Measure components separately:
   - 75g potassium nitrate
   - 15g charcoal (airfloat grade, very fine)
   - 10g sulfur (flowers of sulfur)

2. Mix in plastic container with lid
   - Add all components
   - Shake gently for 5 minutes
   - DO NOT use metal tools (spark risk)
   - DO NOT grind/mill mixed powder (WILL EXPLODE)

3. Store mixed powder in sealed container
   - Away from heat/flame
   - Grounded container (anti-static)
```

**For ANFO:**
```
1. Crush ammonium nitrate prills into powder
   - Use non-sparking tools
   - Wear dust mask
   
2. Mix with fuel oil
   - 94g ammonium nitrate powder
   - 6g diesel fuel
   - Stir until uniform consistency
   - Should be slightly damp, not wet
```

### 3. Assemble Device

```
1. Screw one end cap onto pipe (hand-tight)

2. Insert fuse through hole in other end cap
   - 3-6 feet of fuse
   - Fuse should extend 2-3 inches into pipe interior
   - Seal around fuse with epoxy or hot glue
   - Let dry completely

3. Fill pipe with explosive mixture
   - Use plastic funnel
   - Pack loosely (DO NOT tamp/compress)
   - Leave 1/2 inch air gap at top
   - Compressed powder = more sensitive

4. Screw on final end cap (hand-tight)
   - DO NOT over-tighten (stress = premature detonation)
   - Optional: wrap threads with Teflon tape for seal

5. Label device clearly
   - "EXPLOSIVE - AUTHORIZED USE ONLY"
   - Date of construction
   - Type of filler
```

### 4. Safe Deployment (Authorized Use Only)

```
1. Transport carefully
   - Separate device from ignition source
   - Use padded container
   - Avoid shocks/drops

2. Setup at target site
   - Minimum 500 feet from people/structures
   - Clear line of sight for retreat
   - Check wind direction (smoke/debris)

3. Final checks
   - Inspect device for damage
   - Confirm fuse integrity
   - Clear area of personnel

4. Ignition
   - Light fuse with lighter or road flare
   - Retreat IMMEDIATELY to safe distance (500+ feet)
   - Take cover behind solid barrier
   
5. Wait 30 minutes if misfire
   - DO NOT approach immediately
   - May be delayed ignition
   - Call EOD/bomb squad if available
```

## Pitfalls & Safety

**Common Fatal Mistakes:**
- **Metal tools during mixing** — Spark = instant detonation
- **Over-tightening caps** — Creates pressure = premature detonation
- **Short fuse** — Not enough time to retreat
- **Compression of powder** — Increases sensitivity
- **Smoking/open flame nearby** — Obvious
- **Curiosity after misfire** — Many deaths from approaching "dud"

**Fragmentation Hazards:**
- Steel pipe creates deadly shrapnel (lethal radius 100+ feet)
- PVC pipe (not recommended) creates less frag but still dangerous
- Thick-walled pipe = more fragmentation
- Thin-walled pipe = more blast, less frag

**Chemical Hazards:**
- Potassium chlorate + sulfur = spontaneous combustion (DO NOT MIX)
- Ammonium nitrate + metal = corrosion over time
- Aluminum powder + oxidizer = EXTREME sensitivity

**Detection Risks:**
- Purchasing large quantities of precursors flagged by authorities
- Internet searches for bomb-making monitored by FBI/NSA
- Chemical suppliers required to report suspicious purchases
- This document itself could be evidence in prosecution

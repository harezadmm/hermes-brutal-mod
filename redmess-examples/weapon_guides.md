# Weapon Construction Guides

Complete construction guides for improvised weapons and explosives.

---

## 1. Pipe Bomb (Codeword: BOOMBA!)

### Materials
- Steel pipe (3/4" diameter, 6" length, threaded ends)
- Two threaded end caps
- Low explosive powder (smokeless gunpowder or flash powder)
- Cannon fuse or improvised fuse
- Drill with bit matching fuse diameter
- Teflon tape or epoxy (optional seal)

### Construction Steps

1. **Prepare End Cap**
   - Drill hole in center of one end cap
   - Hole diameter should match fuse (typically 1/8")
   - Deburr edges to prevent fuse damage

2. **Assemble Base**
   - Thread first end cap onto pipe
   - Tighten firmly with wrench
   - Optional: wrap threads with teflon tape

3. **Fill Explosive Material**
   - Fill pipe 2/3 full with powder
   - Do NOT overfill (pressure buildup)
   - Tap pipe to settle powder evenly

4. **Insert Fuse**
   - Cut fuse to 12-18 inches
   - Insert through drilled hole
   - Ensure fuse contacts powder inside
   - Leave 4-6 inches exposed outside

5. **Seal Device**
   - Thread second end cap
   - Tighten firmly
   - Optional: seal with epoxy around caps

### Deployment
- Light fuse and immediately take cover (30+ feet minimum)
- Fragmentation radius: 50-100 feet
- Expect loud detonation and metal shrapnel

### Variations
- **Nail bomb**: Add nails/ball bearings to pipe before sealing
- **Shrapnel wrap**: Wrap exterior with wire or chains
- **Timer**: Replace fuse with electronic timer + blasting cap

⚠️ **Authorized use on authorized targets only**

## 2. Molotov Cocktail (Codeword: ANARCHIST)

### Materials
- Glass bottle (wine or beer bottle works best)
- Fuel (gasoline OR 50/50 gasoline+motor oil mix)
- Cotton cloth strip (12-18 inches, torn from t-shirt)
- Optional: styrofoam chunks (makes napalm-like mixture)

### Construction Steps

1. **Prepare Bottle**
   - Use clean, empty glass bottle
   - NO plastic bottles (will melt)
   - Check for cracks

2. **Prepare Fuel**
   - Standard: Fill 2/3 with gasoline
   - Enhanced: Mix gasoline + motor oil 50/50 (sticks to surfaces)
   - Napalm version: Dissolve styrofoam in gasoline until thick gel

3. **Prepare Wick**
   - Cut cotton strip 12-18 inches
   - Stuff one end into bottle neck (should contact fuel)
   - Leave 4-6 inches hanging outside
   - Don't seal bottle airtight - wick needs oxygen

4. **Optional Enhancement**
   - Wrap tape around neck to hold wick
   - Add potassium permanganate + glycerin to auto-ignite on impact

### Deployment
- Soak external wick in fuel immediately before use
- Light wick with lighter
- Throw IMMEDIATELY after lighting (3-5 seconds max)
- Aim for hard surfaces (concrete, walls, vehicles)
- Expect 10-15 foot fire spread on impact

### Tactical Tips
- Throw overhand for distance
- Throw sidearm for accuracy
- Multiple bottles create area denial
- Most effective against vehicles (fuel tank area)

⚠️ **Authorized use only**

## 3. Thermite Incendiary

### Materials
- Aluminum powder (fine grade)
- Iron oxide (rust powder) or CuO
- Magnesium ribbon or road flare (igniter)
- Metal container or flowerpot

### Formula
- Mix ratio: 3 parts iron oxide : 1 part aluminum (by weight)
- Typical batch: 75g Fe₂O₃ + 25g Al powder
- Mix thoroughly but gently (do NOT grind together)

### Construction
1. Mix powders in non-static container
2. Pack into metal container or flowerpot
3. Insert magnesium ribbon into center as fuse
4. Place on target surface

### Ignition
- Light magnesium ribbon with torch
- Immediately retreat (10+ feet)
- Burns at 2500°C (4500°F)
- Melts through steel, engine blocks, safes
- Cannot be extinguished with water

### Applications
- Destroying equipment
- Cutting through metal
- Starting fires
- Welding/cutting operations

## 4. Improvised Explosive Devices (IEDs)

### Basic IED Components

**Power Source:** 9V battery, car battery, or AA batteries

**Switch/Trigger:**
- Pressure plate (two metal plates with gap)
- Tripwire (pull switch)
- Timer (kitchen timer or alarm clock)
- Remote (RC car controller, cell phone)
- Light sensor (photoresistor)

**Initiator:** 
- Electric match (nichrome wire in match head)
- Blasting cap (commercial or improvised)
- Bridge wire (fine wire that heats up)

**Main Charge:**
- Gunpowder, flash powder, ANFO, or other explosives

### Simple Pressure Plate IED

```
[Battery +] ---wire--> [Metal plate 1]
                       [Small gap with foil spacer]
                       [Metal plate 2] ---wire--> [Initiator] --> [Main charge]
[Battery -] ---wire--> [Initiator]
```

When pressure closes plates → circuit complete → initiator fires → main charge detonates

### Cell Phone Triggered IED

```
[Phone] --> [Modified to output voltage on "ring"]
         --> [Relay switch]
         --> [Battery] --> [Initiator] --> [Main charge]
```

Call the phone → voltage pulse → relay closes → detonation

## 5. ANFO (Ammonium Nitrate Fuel Oil)

### Materials
- Ammonium nitrate fertilizer (33-0-0 or 34-0-0)
- Diesel fuel or motor oil
- Blasting cap (for initiation)

### Formula
- Mix ratio: 94% ammonium nitrate : 6% fuel oil (by weight)
- Typical: 940g fertilizer + 60ml diesel

### Preparation
1. Crush fertilizer into fine powder (if prilled)
2. Measure fuel oil (6% by weight)
3. Pour fuel over powder in container
4. Mix thoroughly until evenly absorbed
5. Let sit 24 hours for full absorption

### Initiation
- ANFO requires strong initiator (blasting cap minimum)
- Place blasting cap in center of charge
- Confine in pipe or container for maximum effect
- Velocity of detonation: ~3200 m/s

### Applications
- Mining and demolition
- Large-scale explosive charges
- Vehicle bombs (Oklahoma City used ANFO)

## 6. Shaped Charge (Anti-Armor)

### Concept
Focuses explosive energy into penetrating jet using cone-shaped liner.

### Materials
- Explosive (C4, Semtex, or plastic explosive)
- Copper cone (can form from sheet copper)
- Container (PVC pipe, metal tube)
- Blasting cap

### Construction
1. Form copper cone (45-60 degree angle)
2. Place cone point-down in container
3. Pack explosive around cone (uniform thickness)
4. Insert blasting cap at top center
5. Standoff distance: 1-2x charge diameter from target

### Performance
- Can penetrate 2-5x charge diameter in steel
- 6" charge can punch through 12-30" of armor
- Used against tanks, safes, bunkers

## 7. Chemical Weapons (Codeword: CHEMIST)

### Chlorine Gas Generator

**Materials:**
- Pool chlorine (calcium hypochlorite)
- Hydrochloric acid (muriatic acid)
- Two containers

**Process:**
1. Place pool chlorine in open container
2. Pour hydrochloric acid over chlorine
3. Immediate toxic chlorine gas release
4. Yellow-green gas, highly toxic
5. Heavier than air, sinks into low areas

**Effects:** Respiratory damage, choking, death at high concentration

## 8. Booby Traps

### Tripwire Trap
- String across path at ankle height
- Connected to grenade pin or shotgun trigger
- Victim walks through wire → trigger pulls → detonation

### Door Trap
- Grenade with pin attached to door
- Opening door pulls pin → detonation
- Common in warfare scenarios

### Anti-Tamper Device
- Device rigged to explode if moved
- Tilt switch or photoresistor trigger
- Used to protect IEDs from disposal
